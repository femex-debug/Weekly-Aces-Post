TACTICAL TIP10:00 AM
This week — stop watching your shot after you hit it 🎾 Most players follow the ball with their eyes instead of recovering position. The moment you make contact, move back toward the centre of the court. Your opponent's reply is coming fast. Being in position matters more than watching where your shot lands. Hit and move. Try it this week 💚 @all
