TACTICAL TIP OF THE WEEKUse This in Your Next Match10:00 AM
Tactical tip for your next match 🎾 Stop going for winners too early in the rally. Most points in recreational tennis are not won — they are lost. Someone misses before the rally gets interesting. Instead of trying to end the point on shot three, extend the rally. Make your opponent hit one more ball. Then one more. The player who is patient wins more points than the player who is powerful. Try it in your next match this week and see what happens 💚 @all
