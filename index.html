TACTICAL TIP + LOG11:00 AM
This week's tip — serve placement over power 🎾 Pick a target before you start your motion. Wide, body or T. Hit it at 70% with full intent. A consistent serve that lands beats a hard one that clips the net every time. Control first. Power is earned. --- Played a match?
