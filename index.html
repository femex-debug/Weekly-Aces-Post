TACTICAL TIP10:00 AM
Try this in your next match 🎾 Stop going for winners too early in the rally. Most points at our level are lost — not won. Someone misses before anything interesting happens. Instead of trying to end the point on shot three, just extend the rally. Make your opponent hit one more ball. Then one more. Patient players beat powerful ones more often than you think. Try it this week 💚 @all
