TACTICAL TIP + LOG11:00 AM
This week's tip — recover to centre after every shot 🎾 The moment you make contact, move back to the centre mark. Not after you watch where your ball lands — immediately after contact. Your opponent's next shot is already coming. Hit. Move. Reset. That rhythm wins points. --- Played a match this week?
